# Insights sheet

See the big picture across everything you track — accounts, savings,
items, properties, and loans — on one sheet, starting with your net
worth.

## Net worth

The headline figure is everything you own minus everything you owe:

- your **account balances**,
- your **savings** balances,
- the current value of the **items** you own (depreciation applied),
- the value of your **properties**,
- minus their **mortgages**,
- minus your other **loans**.

A breakdown below the total shows what each category contributes, and a
chart draws your net worth over time — sampled monthly from the
earliest dated data you have through today, so the line ends exactly at
the figure above it. Time-range buttons below the chart (1Y / 2Y / 3Y /
5Y / All — the same row as the loans chart) zoom the line to a recent
window or stretch it across everything you've recorded.

A loan that is linked to a property's mortgage is never counted twice:
the mortgage counts with its property, and the linked loan steps aside.
Anything without a recorded value yet shows as "—" and simply doesn't
contribute.

## Choose what counts

Open **Net worth settings** from the sheet's "…" menu to tailor the
roll-up:

- **Exclude** anything that shouldn't count — a child's account, a
  collection item you'd never sell, a property held for someone else.
- Set an **ownership share** for anything you don't own alone. A house
  co-owned 50/50 contributes half its value — and half its mortgage —
  so the figure is your share of the equity. An account shared with a
  spouse works the same way.

Each row in the settings shows its current contribution live, so you
can see the effect of a toggle or a share before saving. Loans linked
to a property's mortgage follow that property's setting and aren't
listed separately.

## More modes to come

The Insights sheet is built around insight modes. Net worth is the
first; future modes will slice the same data from other angles, and a
mode switcher will appear at the top of the sheet once there's more
than one.
