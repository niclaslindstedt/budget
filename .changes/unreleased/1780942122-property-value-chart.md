Visualize a property's value over time. A new **Visualize value** entry in a property's "…" menu charts its recorded values as a line graph, with toggles to fold in the money spent on repairs and to overlay the net value you'd actually take home after broker, advertising, repairs, purchase price, and capital-gains tax. The chart follows your theme — colours, font, corners, and spacing all match.
