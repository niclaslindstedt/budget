See what having a car really costs — owned, leased, shared, or via a car pool — by linking its charges from your bank history (within the dates you owned it), logging its value and range (odometer readings in kilometres or miles, your pick next to Location in Settings) as often as you like — or, for a car pool with no odometer of its own, the distance of each trip recorded right on the expense — attaching the purchase, leasing, or sale contract, and rolling everything up to a cost per distance against a monthly-average line; a leased car's changing equity even moves your net worth, negative while it's underwater early in the term.
