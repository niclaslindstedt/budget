Currency presets in Settings → Format: pick from Nordic (SEK, NOK,
DKK, ISK), European (EUR, GBP, CHF) or North American (USD, CAD)
without spelling out the symbol, position, and spacing by hand. The
custom-token inputs stay available behind a "Custom…" entry. Fresh
installs default to a preset matching the browser's region.
