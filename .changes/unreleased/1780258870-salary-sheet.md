A new **Salary** sheet type that shows your pay over time. **Find
salaries** scans a chosen account's full bank history — even years back —
finds your recurring paycheck, establishes a baseline, and walks you
through it year by year so you can accept, edit, or skip each month (it
prefers recurring income over big one-offs and flags likely job changes).
Tag each salary with an **employer** and role, and record taxes and
absence days (parental leave, VAB, vacation, sick). Salaries are listed
per year with gross and net totals.
