A new **Salary** sheet type that shows your pay over time. Detect likely
salaries from your budget with **Find salaries** (it picks one paycheck a
month, preferring recurring income over big one-offs, and flags likely job
changes), tag each with an **employer** and role, and record taxes and
absence days (parental leave, VAB, vacation, sick). Salaries are listed
per year with gross and net totals.
