**Save as** menu in the account-history modal (the modal you reach
from the eye icon on an account row). Exports the entire imported
history as PDF, Excel (XLSX), or CSV — same dropdown style and the
same three formats as the budget sheet's view-mode modal. The
exported CSV and XLSX files are also recognised by the bank-import
flow: pick one back through the Import button and the importer
deduplicates it against your existing entries, so a round-trip
through the file is safe.
