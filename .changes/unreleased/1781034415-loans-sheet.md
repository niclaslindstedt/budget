Track the money you owe — student loans, car loans, mortgages (link one or several of a property's mortgages from the Properties sheet as a single loan), private and personal loans — record the outstanding balance as of a date, and import payments from your bank transactions so the remaining balance follows along: mark one charge with the loan's type and the import suggests the rest by bank description and similar amount, optionally tags and renames them for you, and attaches matching charges automatically on future imports.
