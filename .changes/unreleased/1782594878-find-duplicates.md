---
type: Added
title: Find duplicates
---

The Accounts sheet's ⋯ menu now has **Find duplicates**, which scans every account's bank history for the same transaction imported into more than one account, pre-selects the account it belongs to (by reconstructing each account's running-balance chain and spotting the copy that doesn't fit), and lets you accept all the suggestions in one click to remove the stray copies.
