---
type: Added
title: Find duplicates
---

The Accounts sheet's ⋯ menu now has **Find duplicates**, which scans every account's bank history for the same transaction — same date, description, amount, and running balance — imported into more than one account, pre-selects the account it belongs to, and lets you accept all the suggestions in one click to remove the stray copies; tap any match to see the surrounding bank history and balances, or **Ignore** a legitimate recurring charge so it's never flagged again.
