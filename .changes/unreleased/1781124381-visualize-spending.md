type: Added
title: Visualize spending
---

The budget sheet's … menu gains a dashboard of where your money actually went (completed entries and imported bank history only): monthly bars stacked per category, a donut you can click to drill into a category's types, income against expenses month by month, and your top merchants, all over a 3M / 6M / 12M / All window. Give an owned item a **Lifetime** in the item editor and the dashboard's cogwheel option spreads its cost evenly across that lifetime — Swedish "avskrivning" — so big purchases stop spiking the month they happened.
