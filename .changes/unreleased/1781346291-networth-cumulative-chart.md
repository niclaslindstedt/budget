title: Net worth over time
---

The Insights net-worth chart stacks each category as its own band — assets above a zero line, other loans below it, and a line tracing your total — with a property's mortgages folded into its band so the two figures that dwarf everything else move together. Tick a band off in the legend to drop it from the stack and rescale the chart, so the smaller balances become readable.
