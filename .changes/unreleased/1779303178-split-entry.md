New scissors action button on every row opens a Split modal that
carves the entry into separate parts — handy for a mortgage payment
that's part interest and part amortization, or a bankgiro
transaction that paid for several unrelated things in one go. Each
split becomes its own row on the same date; any leftover stays on
the original row (preserving its recurring-series link) and is
pushed to the bottom of the list.
