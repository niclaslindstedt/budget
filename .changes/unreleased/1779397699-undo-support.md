Undo and redo support for every edit. The last 50 actions can be
stepped backward and forward via ⌘Z / ⌘⇧Z (or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y on
Windows), or the new buttons next to the save indicator. A third
button next to undo / redo opens an **Action history** modal that
lists every action with its timestamp so you can jump straight to a
known-good point instead of guessing how many times to press undo.
Future actions stay visible (greyed) after you go back so you can
redo into them; they're cleared only when you take a new action.
