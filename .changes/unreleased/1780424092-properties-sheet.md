New **Properties** sheet — track the homes and apartments you own: what
each was bought for, its size, its value over time (record a new value
any time to add a point to its history), and the mortgages against it.
For each mortgage you can record the lender (a company), the sum you
borrowed, the current balance, the interest rate, how often the rate
resets, when it next changes, and the monthly amortisation — either a
percentage of the initial loan or a fixed sum per month. Bind a mortgage
to a bank account and "Find mortgage payments" pulls its payments from
that account's history: it homes in on the charges you tagged with the
mortgage's lender or the Mortgage type, learns their bank description, and
sweeps the rest of the history for every matching month — ranking the
likeliest charges first and leaving a previous home's loan out by its
different amount. Tick the charges to record and it adds every matching
month, showing how many payments across how many months before you
confirm. A property can carry several mortgages. Choose whether sizes read
as "kvm" or "sqm" in the Property settings tab.
