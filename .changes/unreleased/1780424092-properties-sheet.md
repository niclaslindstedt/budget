New **Properties** sheet — track the homes and apartments you own: what
each was bought for, its value over time (record a new value any time
to add a point to its history), and the mortgages against it. Bind a
mortgage to a bank account and "Find mortgage payments" scans its
history for the recurring charge, recording each month's amortisation
and interest. A property can carry several mortgages.
